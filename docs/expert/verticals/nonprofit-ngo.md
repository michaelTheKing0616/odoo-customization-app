# Vertical playbook: Nonprofit / NGO

Keywords: nonprofit, NGO, charity, donation, grant, fundraising, volunteer, donor.

## Summary

Nonprofits center on **Contacts** (donors, volunteers, beneficiaries), **CRM** (grants pipeline),
**Events** (fundraising galas), **Survey** (applications), **Website** (campaigns), and
**Accounting** (restricted funds — configure carefully with an accountant).

## Stock apps

`base`, `contacts`, `mail`, `crm`, `website`, `event`, `survey`, `sale` (donation products),
`account`, `project` (programs optional).

## Custom models

`x_grant`, `x_program`, `x_donation_pledge` when CRM opportunities are too sales-oriented.
Link donations to partners and analytic accounts for fund tracking.

## Workflows

Campaign → Event registration → Donation product on invoice or payment link. Grant tracking via CRM stages or custom grant model.

## Compliance

Expert does not provide legal/tax advice on charitable status — describe Odoo capabilities only.
